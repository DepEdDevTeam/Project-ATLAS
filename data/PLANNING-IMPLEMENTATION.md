## Hazard connector implementation

School-level hazard assessment JSON can now be imported through `/api/agents/hazards` and selected in the planning UI. The connector requires an original HTTPS source URL, release, geography, period, hazard definition, expected inventory coverage and unique school IDs with true/false/null classifications. Snapshots are immutable, hashed and integrity-checked when loaded. Runs retain the actual snapshot and include its source in all Environmental results, timelines and alternatives. Assessed-school counts are distinct from scope-wide exposure totals: missing or unassessed schools prevent scope-wide totals. Imported classifications are conditional provider evidence, not independently certified observations. Automated tests cover missing classifications, partial coverage, mismatched geography, duplicate IDs, source integrity and restart persistence. No real external hazard assessment has been imported or independently verified yet.
